# 🎯 Axiom Shield: Build Strategy Decision Matrix

## Option A: Standalone Extension (RECOMMENDED)
**Build it completely separate from your deterministic engine**

### ✅ Pros
1. **Faster to market** - No coupling complexity
2. **Cleaner demo** - "Install and it just works"
3. **Different customer** - Safety buyers ≠ workflow automation buyers
4. **Portfolio diversification** - Two revenue streams
5. **Easier to sell** - Chrome Web Store listing = passive installs

### ⚠️ Cons
1. No immediate synergy with existing codebase
2. Two products to maintain

### 💰 Business Model
- **Freemium:** Basic PII redaction is free
- **Pro Tier ($9/mo):** Custom regex patterns + audit logs
- **Enterprise ($49/mo):** Sync logs to your deterministic engine (future integration point)

---

## Option B: Integrated Feature
**Build it as a module within your deterministic business webapp**

### ✅ Pros
1. **Unified platform** - One product to market
2. **Data synergy** - Redaction logs feed into your audit trail
3. **Upsell path** - Existing customers adopt safety features

### ⚠️ Cons
1. **Scope explosion** - Browser extension + webapp integration = 3x complexity
2. **Demo friction** - "You need to sign up AND install an extension"
3. **Delayed launch** - Integration work adds 2-4 weeks
4. **Mixed value prop** - Confuses messaging ("Are you a workflow tool or a safety tool?")

---

## 🏆 The Verdict: Build Standalone

### Why?
Your deterministic engine is about **process automation**.
Axiom Shield is about **risk mitigation**.

**These are different jobs-to-be-done.**

### The Integration Path (Later)
**Phase 1 (Today):** Launch Axiom Shield as a standalone Chrome extension.

**Phase 2 (Month 2):** Add an "Export Logs" button that generates a CSV of all redactions.

**Phase 3 (Month 3):** Offer an Enterprise tier where logs auto-sync to your deterministic engine's audit trail.

**This way:**
- You validate the AI Safety market independently
- Your deterministic engine stays focused on its core value prop
- Integration becomes an **upsell**, not a dependency
